- Patch: `benchmarks/multi_node/srt-slurm-recipes/configs/patches/vllm-separate-cudagraph-pools.patch`

## What is patched

The patch replaces the global CUDA graph pool used by full-model graph capture with a private
`torch.cuda.graph_pool_handle()`. Breakable piecewise graphs continue to use the platform global pool.

## Why the unmodified image cannot run this benchmark

With Kimi-K3 TP8/DCP8, Mooncake cache eviction, and full CUDA graphs, sharing the global pool between
full and piecewise captures causes a CUDA memory fault and leaves the server hung. The unmodified
minimal reproducer faulted in four of four runs. The private-pool change completed five of five matched
runs, five of five reduced runs, five of five larger-cache runs, and five of five AgentX runs.
