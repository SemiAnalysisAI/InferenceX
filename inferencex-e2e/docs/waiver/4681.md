# Inference-engine patch waiver — PR #4681

Filed per [`docs/PR_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md`](../PR_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md) (image-provenance / patch item): the setup script
of the disaggregated recipe installs EFA transport components before serving. Nothing is patched or built: vLLM is
the pinned nightly, libfabric comes from the AWS EFA installer, NIXL is the released 1.5.2 wheel, and Mooncake is the
0.3.14rc3 wheel from its `v0.3.14-pre-3` pre-release.

## What the setup installs

The setup follows the EFA-installer step of vLLM's `docker/Dockerfile.efa`
([vllm-project/vllm#58768](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/58768))
on the pinned nightly. In place of that image's Mooncake EFA wheel at the base image's version, it installs the
Mooncake 0.3.14rc3 wheel, and it installs the NIXL 1.5.2 wheel because the image's 1.5.0 fails on libfabric 2.7. Unlike
that image, the disaggregated recipe keeps `NCCL_NET_PLUGIN=none` (as the GB300 disaggregated recipes do) and leaves
DeepEP as the pinned image ships it.

- EFA userspace exactly as `Dockerfile.efa` installs it: AWS EFA installer 1.51.0 (same SHA256) with `--skip-mpi
  --skip-rdma-core --build-ngc`, i.e. libfabric 2.7.0amzn2.0 and the NGC aws-ofi-nccl plugin. The disaggregated
  workers set `NCCL_TUNER_PLUGIN=ofi`, as that image does.
- Mooncake `mooncake-transfer-engine-cuda13` 0.3.14rc3, the cp312 x86_64 wheel attached to the kvcache-ai/Mooncake
  `v0.3.14-pre-3` pre-release (SHA256-pinned), replacing the image's Mooncake, until 0.3.14 is on PyPI. Since
  kvcache-ai/Mooncake#4356 the default wheels include the EFA transport; the setup checks `engine.SUPPORT_EFA`. The
  pre-release carries the THP-backed EFA host segments (kvcache-ai/Mooncake#4393) and the EFA handshake lock fix
  (#4704) that the latest release, 0.3.13.post1, lacks.
- NIXL 1.5.2, the released `nixl-cu13` wheel from PyPI (SHA256-pinned), replacing the image's 1.5.0. Its LIBFABRIC
  plugin loads the installer's libfabric 2.7. Since 1.5.0 the plugin gained the fallback for context-independent
  CUDA allocations (ai-dynamo/nixl#2425, backport #2470, in 1.5.1), and 1.5.2 disables EFA unsolicited write receive
  (#2494, backport #2529) and serializes endpoint, CQ and AV calls with the rail endpoint lock (#2542).
- Ubuntu runtime packages: the system libraries the Mooncake wheel links against (libibverbs with its providers,
  libnuma, libcurl), libnl for the installer's libfabric, and libhwloc15 for aws-ofi-nccl.
- `NIXL_DISABLE_CUDA_ADDR_WA=1` and the Mooncake/libfabric EFA environment in the recipe (configuration).

The installer tarball and the wheels are cached under the runner's mounted Hugging Face cache behind an exclusive
lock once their SHA256 matches; nothing on the host (drivers, huge-page settings, network configuration) is
modified.

## Eager-mode prefill (configuration, not a patch)

- The disaggregated prefill workers set `enforce-eager: true`, as #4304 does, in place of the prefill CUDA graph
  settings of #4516. At the pinned vLLM, `enforce-eager` also turns off torch.compile and JIT kernel warmup on those
  workers, so prefill runs vLLM's custom-op kernels without Inductor fusion, and JIT-compiled kernels build on first
  use instead of at startup. The decode workers keep torch.compile and vLLM's default CUDA graphs.
- Weights, the attention and MoE backends, the draft model and synthetic acceptance are unchanged.

## Why the unmodified upstream image cannot run this benchmark

- The pinned image has no EFA transport. The vLLM EFA image is defined upstream (vllm-project/vllm#58768) but not yet
  published to Docker Hub (vllm-project/vllm#64028), and the Mooncake release it needs (0.3.14) is not out yet: the
  released Mooncake allocator cannot register the per-rank KV segment over EFA. The pinned image also ships NIXL
  1.5.0, which aborted KV transfers on libfabric 2.7 in our runs. vLLM moved its pin to 1.5.2 in
  vllm-project/vllm#64579 (merged 2026-10-31), after the pinned nightly.

## Removal plan

- EFA setup: pin the published vLLM EFA image once it carries Mooncake 0.3.14 and NIXL 1.5.2, and drop the setup
  script.
- Eager prefill: drop `enforce-eager` once host-launched CUDA graphs run stably on this cluster, or once a pinned
  vLLM image offers device-launched graphs (vllm-project/vllm#63869).
