# PRINCIPLES draft

InferenceX is the open platform for comparing ai accelerators to accelerators

- Equitablenes towards AI accelerators
- Positive Industry Wide Impact while *"doing as much as needed, as little as possible"*
- move fast, break things, fix fast


## Anti-Goal
- Compare inference engines. Unfortunately due to don't have enough GPU time or upstream maintainer bandwidth to compare many inference engines, for those that want to compare inference engines, we recommend forking the repo, hooking up self hosted GPU resources to your fork repo and potentially writing your own blog on it similar to [ParaSail Engine](https://www.parasail.io/blog/minimax-m3-1-flash-day-0)
